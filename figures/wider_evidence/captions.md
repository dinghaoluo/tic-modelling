# Figure captions

## parameter_distributions
Parameter distributions for Ramp-down TICs (n = 404), Ramp-up TICs (n = 479), and Time cells (n = 1177). Panels A and B show fitted Gaussian width, σ, and time constant, τ, respectively. The histograms include all cells, including bound cells. Bound values are σ = 4 s and τ = 10 s and are assigned to the final ≥ bin. The accompanying p-values in `wider_parameter_summary.md` are two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on session-level medians comparing each TIC group with Time cells, with sessions as the unit and n = 15 sessions. Peak reference conventions used in the companion fitted-parameter summaries are last-lick reference for Ramp-down TICs and Time cells and time before the next lick for Ramp-up TICs, with negative values before the next lick.

## receptive_field_model_summary
Summary of model decisions and fitted parameters for Ramp-down TICs (n = 404), Ramp-up TICs (n = 479), and Time cells (n = 1177). Every p-value in panels A and B is a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test on session-level decision contrasts, with sessions as the unit and n = 15 sessions. The p-value in panel C tests the Ramp-down TIC versus Time-cell peak-location difference with the same two-sided paired session-level test, n = 15 sessions. The Ramp-up TIC versus Time-cell peak comparison is omitted because the two peak references differ. The p-values in panels D and E test each TIC group against Time cells using two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on session-level medians, with sessions as the unit and n = 15 sessions. Bound cells are included in the plotted medians and tests and marked with crosses. Bound values are σ = 4 s and τ = 10 s. Ramp-down TIC and Time-cell peaks are referenced to the last lick. Ramp-up TIC peaks are reported as time before the next lick, with negative values before the next lick.

## published_parameter_comparison
Comparison of fitted parameters for Ramp-down TICs (n = 404) and Ramp-up TICs (n = 479) with published values. Fitted boxes include all cells, including bound cells. Bound values are σ = 4 s and τ = 10 s and are marked with crosses in the summary boxplots. The p-values in `paper_parameter_comparison.md` test each fitted TIC group against the corresponding Cao et al. 2024 published median using two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on session-level medians, with sessions as the unit and n = 15 sessions. Cao et al. 2024 boxes show published medians and interquartile ranges. Ramp-down TIC peaks are referenced to the last lick. Ramp-up TIC peaks are reported as time before the next lick, with negative values before the next lick.
