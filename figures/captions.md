# Figure captions

## parameter_distributions
The fitting window runs from 1 s before the last lick to the first lick of the next trial. Parameter distributions for Ramp-down TICs (n = 404), Ramp-up TICs (n = 479), and Time cells (n = 1177). Panels A and B show fitted Gaussian width, sigma, and time constant, tau, respectively. The histograms include all cells, including bound cells. Bound values are sigma = 4 s and tau = 10 s and are assigned to the final >= bin. The accompanying p-values in wider_parameter_summary.md are two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on session-level medians comparing each TIC group with Time cells, with sessions as the unit and n = 15 sessions.

## receptive_field_model_summary
The fitting window runs from 1 s before the last lick to the first lick of the next trial. Summary of model decisions and fitted parameters for Ramp-down TICs (n = 404), Ramp-up TICs (n = 479), and Time cells (n = 1177). Panels A and B show per-cell held-out decisions. Each p-value is a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test of session medians of the held-out log-likelihood contrast against zero (Gaussian minus exponential in A; curved minus linear in B), n = 15 sessions. Panel C uses one shared last-lick reference: Ramp-down TICs use decay_mu, Time cells use gaussian_mu, and Ramp-up TICs use the session median observed trial interval T_k minus rise_mu. Its three p-values test Ramp-down TICs versus Time cells, Ramp-up TICs versus Time cells, and Ramp-down TICs versus Ramp-up TICs with two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on session medians, n = 15 sessions. The p-values in panels D and E test each TIC group against Time cells using two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on session-level medians, with sessions as the unit and n = 15 sessions. Bound cells are included in the plotted medians and tests and marked with crosses. Bound values are sigma = 4 s and tau = 10 s.

## published_parameter_comparison
The fitting window runs from 1 s before the last lick to the first lick of the next trial. Comparison of fitted parameters for Ramp-down TICs (n = 404) and Ramp-up TICs (n = 479) with published values. TIC boxes show the median and IQR with whiskers at 1.5 x IQR, with bound cells included at tau = 10 s. The p-values in paper_parameter_comparison.md are two-sided one-sample Wilcoxon signed-rank tests of session medians against the corresponding published median, treated as fixed, n = 15 sessions. Cao et al. 2024 boxes show published medians and IQRs only. Cao et al. 2024 excluded past cells with tau > 9 s (16/331).
